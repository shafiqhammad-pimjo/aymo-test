# Aymo AI Usage Guidelines

Aymo AI helps with writing, research, coding, and everyday tasks. Use these guidelines to get useful results and review them responsibly.

## How to use Aymo AI

1. **State your goal clearly.** Explain what you want Aymo AI to do and provide relevant context.
2. **Include useful details.** Specify the audience, desired format, constraints, or examples where helpful.
3. **Iterate.** Ask follow-up questions or request revisions to refine the result.
4. **Verify important information.** AI responses may be inaccurate or outdated. Check facts, code, and recommendations before relying on them.
5. **Protect sensitive information.** Avoid sharing passwords, access tokens, confidential business information, or unnecessary personal data.
6. **Review generated code.** Test it, check for security issues, and adapt it to your project before use.
7. **Use AI responsibly.** Follow applicable laws, workplace policies, and third-party terms.

## Example prompts

- “Explain this React component and suggest improvements.”
- “Turn these notes into a concise project update for my team.”
- “Help me debug this error. Here’s the relevant code and what I expected.”
- “Summarize this document and list the key action items.”

## Remember

Aymo AI is an assistant, not a substitute for your judgment. You’re responsible for reviewing and deciding how to use its output.
